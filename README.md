### Project 1: Ịmụ-Asụsụ

#### PROBLEM STATEMENT

The histories and cultures of the Global South are under-represented in the digital record, and much of what _has_ been documented sits outside the communities it describes — held in foreign archives and museums, framed from an outside perspective, or carried in oral traditions that are disappearing faster than they are being recorded. The institutions positioned to close this gap are underfunded, fragmented, and rarely built around the people whose history is at stake.

#### VISION STATEMENT

A world where a community's history is recorded by the people who live it, reachable by anyone who wants it, and taught through materials worth learning from.

Ịmụ-Asụsụ is how we start building that record: a platform that holds contributors to scholarly standards, makes collaboration the default rather than the exception, and treats the design of the experience as part of the preservation — not decoration on top of it.
